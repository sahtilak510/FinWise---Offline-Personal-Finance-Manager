PART 1 - MAIN PROGRAM: THE JAVA FILES (src/main/java/com/finance)
================================================================================

--- 1.1 app - the starting point ---------------------------------------------

app/OfflineFinanceApplication.java
    The "on" switch. Starts the whole program and turns on the automatic timers
    (nightly backup, daily repeat transactions).

--- 1.2 config - the rules of the house --------------------------------------

config/SecurityConfig.java
    Decides who may enter. Scrambles passwords safely, leaves the sign-in and
    sign-up pages open, locks everything else behind a signed-in user.

config/WebConfig.java
    Tells the web server where the pages, styles, scripts and images live.

config/WebMvcConfig.java
    Extra web settings, including which checks run before each page opens.

config/AppLockInterceptor.java
    The extra padlock: after signing in, it keeps asking for the app PIN until
    the PIN is typed in the same session.

config/GlobalExceptionHandler.java
    Catches any crash and shows the friendly error page instead of a blank
    screen or a wall of technical text.

config/ChatbotExceptionHandler.java
    Same job, but only for the chatbot, and it answers in a format the web page
    can read.

--- 1.3 controller - the pages (each file controls one screen) ---------------

controller/AuthController.java
    The front door: sign up, sign in, sign out.

controller/HomeController.java
    The main dashboard, and your profile page (change password, colours, language,
    alerts, download all your data, delete the account).

controller/AccountController.java
    Your bank and cash accounts: add, edit, delete, see balances.

controller/IncomeController.java
    Money coming in: list it, search it, filter it, add, edit, delete.

controller/ExpenseController.java
    Money going out: list, search, filter, add, edit, delete, attach and view
    receipt photos.

controller/BudgetController.java
    Spending limits for each category and how close you are to each one.

controller/FinancialGoalController.java
    Savings goals: how much you need, when you want it, how important, done or not.

controller/CategoriesController.java
    Your own categories (like Groceries or Rent). The built-in ones cannot be
    deleted by accident, and you can only change your own.

controller/AnalyticsController.java
    The study page: charts, trends, health score, cash-flow forecast, tips.

controller/ReportController.java
    Makes reports and lets you download them as CSV or Excel.

controller/ImportController.java
    Brings in a bank statement: upload a file, see what was found, fix mistakes,
    then save the records into the app.

controller/ScanController.java
    Scan & Fill: you upload a receipt photo, the app reads it and fills the form
    for you.

controller/NotificationsController.java
    The alerts page: show alerts, mark one read, mark all read, dismiss.

controller/AuditLogController.java
    A diary of what you did: signed in, added, changed, deleted, imported.

controller/SettingsController.java
    Settings page: colours, language, alerts, and the Data & Backup area.

controller/AppLockController.java
    Set, remove or check the app PIN.

controller/ProfileController.java
    A small internal address the pages use to read and update your profile and
    profile photo.

controller/ControllerExceptionHandler.java
    Turns problems on pages into clear messages instead of crashes.

--- 1.4 service - the brains that do the work --------------------------------

-- 1.4a Rule books (these say what a job must do, nothing else) --

service/TransactionService.java
    Rule book: how records (income/expense) are added, changed, found.

service/AnalyticsEngine.java
    Rule book: how totals, trends and charts are worked out.

service/ForecastEngine.java
    Rule book: how next month's income and spending are guessed.

service/CategoryEngine.java
    Rule book: how a purchase is given a category automatically.

service/ReportEngine.java
    Rule book: how a report is put together.

service/DocumentProcessor.java
    Rule book: how an uploaded file becomes a list of records.

service/DocumentScanService.java
    Rule book: how a document is read to get its text.

service/DocumentUploadService.java
    Rule book: how uploaded files are stored and found again.

service/DuplicateDetectionService.java
    Rule book: how repeated records are spotted.

service/OpenCVPreprocessor.java
    Rule book: how a photo is cleaned up before the text is read.

service/Tess4JOcrService.java
    Rule book: how text is read out of a picture.

service/PDFBoxService.java
    Rule book: how text is read out of a PDF.

service/ApachePoiService.java
    Rule book: how Excel files are read and written.

-- 1.4b The real workers (all run on your own computer) --

service/LocalTransactionService.java
    Actually adds, edits, searches and filters your records, only for the
    signed-in user.

service/LocalAnalyticsEngine.java
    Actually works out monthly totals, category shares and trends.

service/LocalForecastEngine.java
    Actually looks at recent months and projects the next ones.

service/LocalCategoryEngine.java
    Actually matches keywords (like "Uber" or "Grocery") to pick a category.

service/LocalReportEngine.java
    Actually collects everything a report or export needs.

service/DefaultDocumentProcessor.java
    Actually sends each file type to the right reader (PDF, picture, CSV, Excel).

service/LocalDocumentScanService.java
    Actually reads PDFs with PDFBox and pictures with text reading, and falls
    back when a file has no readable text.

service/LocalDocumentUploadService.java
    Actually saves uploads outside the web folder with safe made-up names.

service/LocalDuplicateDetectionService.java
    Actually compares amount + date + description to warn "you already have this".

service/LocalOpenCVPreprocessor.java
    Actually makes photos greyer, sharper and straight so the reading works better.

service/LocalTess4JOcrService.java
    Actually runs the text reader and reports the text plus how sure it is.

service/LocalPDFBoxService.java
    Actually pulls the words out of a PDF, page by page.

service/LocalApachePoiService.java
    Actually reads Excel cells into rows and writes Excel files back.

-- 1.4c Day-to-day services --

service/UserService.java
    Creates users, scrambles passwords, updates profiles, changes passwords,
    deletes an account and everything in it, and creates the first admin user.

service/AccountService.java
    The rules for bank and cash accounts and how balances are worked out.

service/IncomeService.java
    The rules for income records, including the search and summary numbers.

service/ExpenseService.java
    The rules for expense records, including receipt photos.

service/BudgetService.java
    Works out how much of each budget is used and warns when you are close.

service/FinancialGoalService.java
    Works out how far along each savings goal is and whether it is done.

service/AppLockService.java
    Stores the app PIN scrambled and checks it when you type it.

service/ChatHistoryService.java
    Remembers what you asked the chatbot, so the chat can continue later.

service/CashFlowForecastService.java
    Builds the "what is coming" view: upcoming bills, likely balance, alerts.

service/TransactionExtractor.java
    Turns the raw text from a file or photo into clean, tidy records.

service/ScanTransactionParser.java
    The clever reader that finds amounts, dates, shop names and categories
    inside messy text.

service/ImportFileService.java
    Checks an uploaded file is the right type and size before reading it.

service/ImportCommitService.java
    Saves the rows you approved, records the import, and marks what was
    rejected or duplicated.

service/UserDataExportService.java
    Packs all your data into one file you can keep or move elsewhere.

service/RecurringTransactionScheduler.java
    Every morning adds the repeat bills and repeat income that are due today,
    and never adds the same one twice.

--- 1.5 service/excel - the Excel copy, alerts, score and backups -------------

service/excel/LocalExcelStorageService.java
    Owns the Excel file Finwise_Data.xlsx: makes it, reads it, writes it.

service/excel/ExcelWorkbookInitializer.java
    Creates that Excel file and all its sheets the very first time you run the app.

service/excel/ExpenseExcelService.java
    Copies your spending into the "Expenses" sheet.

service/excel/IncomeExcelService.java
    Copies your income into the "Income" sheet.

service/excel/BudgetExcelService.java
    Copies your budgets into the "Budgets" sheet.

service/excel/GoalExcelService.java
    Copies your savings goals into the "Goals" sheet.

service/excel/CategoryExcelService.java
    Copies your categories into the "Categories" sheet.

service/excel/NotificationService.java
    Creates the alerts: bill due soon, big purchase, weekly and monthly summaries.

service/excel/FinancialHealthScoreService.java
    Gives your finances a score out of 100 based on your real records: saving
    rate, budget sticking, steadiness of spending, goal progress.

service/excel/SmartInsightsService.java
    Writes helpful tips in plain words, e.g. "food spending is up 18%".

service/excel/BackupRestoreService.java
    Makes a locked (AES-256-GCM) backup file of the database plus the Excel,
    lists old backups, and restores them. Checks file names so nobody can sneak
    a path into a backup, and makes a safety backup before restoring.

service/excel/BackupScheduler.java
    Runs the backup automatically every night at 2:00 and deletes backups older
    than the number of days you chose.

--- 1.6 model - what a record looks like -------------------------------------

model/entity/User.java
    Your account: username, scrambled password, email, name, colour theme,
    language, alert preferences, and when it was created.

model/entity/Account.java
    A bank or cash account: name, type, opening balance, current balance, owner.

model/entity/Income.java
    Money in: amount, date, category, source, description, notes, account,
    receipt, repeat rule, owner.

model/entity/Expense.java
    Money out: same details as income plus payment method and the receipt photo.

model/entity/Budget.java
    A spending limit for one category in one month, with an on/off switch.

model/entity/FinancialGoal.java
    A savings goal: name, target amount, saved so far, target date, status,
    priority.

model/entity/ImportedTransaction.java
    A row waiting for your OK after an import, with the reason if it was
    rejected or flagged as a duplicate.

model/entity/ChatMessage.java
    One message in the chatbot: who said it, what they said, when.

model/dto/UserDTO.java
    A safe copy of your details for display - never includes the password.

model/dto/ExtractedTransactionDTO.java
    One record that was found inside a file or photo, passed along the pipeline.

--- 1.9 chatbot - the offline assistant ---------------------------------------

chatbot/service/ChatbotService.java

--- 1.10 habits - spending pattern checks ------------------------------------

    The promise each check must keep.

    One result from a check: a label, how serious it is, and a tip.

habits/NeedWantRule.java
    Works out how much was needs versus wants.

habits/HostelFoodRule.java
    Spots repeated eating out or food spending.

habits/StreakRule.java
    Finds patterns like spending every day for a week.

habits/HabitService.java
    Runs all the checks and puts their results together.

habits/HabitController.java
    Shows the results on the habits page.

financeplus/service/FinancePlusSettingsService.java

financeplus/service/FinancePlusRecurringService.java

financeplus/service/FinancePlusNotificationService.java

financeplus/service/FinancePlusAttachmentService.java

financeplus/service/FinancePlusBackupService.java

financeplus/service/FinancePlusImportService.java

financeplus/service/FinancePlusExportService.java

Run straight from the code: mvn spring-boot:run
Run the finished program:   java -jar target/offline-finance-manager-1.0.0.jar
Open in your browser:       http://localhost:8080
First sign-in:              admin / admin123  (change it straight away)
Where the data is:          data/finance.db
Where the settings are:     src/main/resources/application.properties
Where the tests write:      target/ (never your data folder)
Where the log is:           logs/finance.log
Where the backups are:      data/backups/
Keep this safe somewhere:   data/backups/.backup.key
